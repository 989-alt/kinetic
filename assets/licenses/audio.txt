## Considered and rejected

- OpenGameArt "Gunshot Sounds" (Raassh, /content/gunshot-sounds): page says CC0 but the bundled `creativecommons.txt` states CC-BY 3.0 (Vincent Sevedge). Conflicting, skipped.
- OpenGameArt "grunts of male death and pain" (thebardofblasphemy, /content/grunts-male-death-and-pain): page license field says CC0, but a comment thread states it "has been licensed GPL". Conflicting, rejected. `sfx_pain` and `sfx_death` are marked `missing` (synth fallback).
- OpenGameArt "Gun Reload Sound Effects" (bmaczero) and "2 gun reloads" (starninjas): not used.
- Freesound / Sonniss: not used (Freesound download needs login; Sonniss license is not CC0).
