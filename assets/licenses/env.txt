# Environment map sources

- night_512.hdr: generated in-house (Task N-06). `tools/blender/render_env_hdr.py` renders the skyline backdrop
  (far-ring cards from GPT, Blender mid-ring towers) with Cycles. No third-party source.
- skyline_atlas_*, skyline_koi_*: derived from GPT-generated panoramas / the TN-12 koi sprite via
  `tools/gpt/skyline_cards.py` (prompts in `tools/gpt/prompts/skyline.md`). sky_win_*, sky_trim_*: procedural
  (`tools/blender/env_skyline_neon.py`).
- dusk_1k.hdr (DEPRECATED, art v3 discards E-01; kept only until the lightmap bake moves to the night
  environment, N-08; `tools/blender/kit_common.py` and `bake_lightmap.py` still read it):
  "Belfast Sunset (Pure Sky)", Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/a/belfast_sunset_puresky).
  Authors: Greg Zaal (processing), Dimitrios Savva (photography), Jarod Guest (sky edits).
  License: CC0 1.0 (https://polyhaven.com/license). 1K HDR, downloaded via api.polyhaven.com
  (https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/hdr/1k/belfast_sunset_puresky_1k.hdr, md5 38890f597727936a44d17a97f7f73354).
